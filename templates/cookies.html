3. WHAT COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES ARE

A cookie is a small text file that is placed on your device (computer, tablet, mobile telephone or other internet-enabled device) when you visit a website. Cookies allow the website to recognise your device and to store certain information about your preferences or past actions. Cookies are widely used to make websites operate, to make them work more efficiently and to provide information to the operators of the website.

Cookies may be:

  • session cookies — which expire when you close your browser; or
  • persistent cookies — which remain on your device for a fixed period or until you delete them.

Cookies may also be:

  • first-party cookies — set by the Website itself; or
  • third-party cookies — set by a third party (such as an analytics or marketing provider) whose service is integrated into the Website.

In this Cookie Policy, references to "cookie" or “cookies” include other technologies that perform similar functions, such as web beacons, pixels, tags, software development kits (SDKs) and local storage.

4. CATEGORIES OF COOKIES WE USE

We use the following categories of cookies on the Website:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: These cookies are essential for the Website to operate. They enable basic functionality such as navigating between pages, accessing secure areas, maintaining session state, recording your cookie preferences and protecting against cross-site request forgery and similar security threats. Without these cookies, services that you have requested cannot be provided. Strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent.
  • Functional cookies: These cookies enable the Website to remember choices you make (such as your language preference) and to provide enhanced or personalised features. Functional cookies are set only with your consent.
  • Performance and analytics cookies: These cookies collect information about how visitors use the Website, such as which pages are most often visited, how long visitors spend on the Website and whether visitors encounter errors. We use this information to understand how the Website is used and to improve its performance and content. Analytics cookies are set only with your consent.
  • Marketing and advertising cookies: These cookies are used to deliver communications relevant to you and your interests, to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and, in some cases, to limit the number of times you see a particular communication. Marketing cookies are set only with your consent. Where these cookies are set by third parties, those third parties may use the information collected to build a profile of your interests and to show you relevant communications on other websites and platforms.

7. YOUR CHOICES AND HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES

You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  • Cookie banner. When you first visit the Website, we present a cookie banner allowing you to accept all cookies, to reject all non-essential cookies, or to make granular choices by category. Strictly necessary cookies are set in all cases, as the Website cannot function without them.
  • Cookie settings. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link on the Website (typically located in the footer). Withdrawing your consent does not affect cookies that have already been set; to remove those, you will need to delete them through your browser as described below.
  • Browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to manage your cookie preferences directly. You can usually set your browser to block all cookies, to accept only first-party cookies, to delete cookies when you close your browser, to be notified when a cookie is set, or to delete cookies that have been set. The procedure differs from browser to browser; please consult the help function of your browser for further information.
  • Opting out of analytics. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available from Google’s website.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of the Website. In particular, if you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the Website may not work properly.
