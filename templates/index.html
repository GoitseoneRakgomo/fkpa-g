About Us

FKPA Global FZCO specializes in physical gold and silver transactions across eligible international markets. Beyond executing the purchase, we help clients answer the practical question that follows: where and how to hold their bullion. Rather than restricting you to a single destination, we match your objectives with vetted independent vaulting operators—including Fort Kobbe Management Corp. in Panama or alternative hubs worldwide—delivering a discreet, tailored advisory experience.

A Central Point of Contact

Cross-border bullion ownership demands careful coordination—from sourcing and insured transit to selecting the right custody hub. We streamline every stage under one roof so you acquire globally, vault securely, and retain direct title throughout.

Services & Transactions We Do Not Offer

To maintain institutional clarity and strict regulatory alignment, FKPA Global FZCO restricts its mandate strictly to physical bullion transactions and independent custody introductions. We do not engage in the following activities:

Financial, Tax, or Legal Advisory

We do not provide personalized investment recommendations, wealth management, portfolio structuring, or legal and tax counsel. All purchasing decisions remain solely with the client and their independent advisors.

Paper Metals & Derivatives

We do not issue or trade unallocated accounts, pooled certificates, futures, options, CFDs, ETFs, or synthetic precious metals exposure. Every transaction involves deliverable, physical bullion.

Direct Vault Operation & Custody

FKPA Global does not own, operate, or manage storage vaults, nor do we hold client assets on our balance sheet. All custody arrangements are contracted directly between the client and accredited third-party vault operators.

Banking, Lending & Leverage

We do not accept cash deposits, operate client escrow or payment accounts, extend credit lines, offer bullion-backed loans, or facilitate margined and leveraged trading.

Retail Walk-Ins & Cash Dealings

We do not operate public retail storefronts, accept physical cash payments, or execute anonymous, unverified, or third-party-funded transactions.

Restricted Jurisdictions & Unverified Sources

We do not transact with sanctioned individuals, entities, or prohibited territories, nor do we source or refine artisanal, uncertified, or non-LBMA/non-accredited material.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I retain direct title to the metals I purchase?

Yes. You hold outright legal ownership of all bullion acquired through FKPA Global, whether delivered directly or held in third-party custody, per your transaction agreement.

How do I open an account?

Submit an enquiry through our contact form to discuss your goals. All prospective buyers must complete standard compliance verification before executing an order.

Why are KYC and AML checks mandatory?

Strict Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and sanctions screening protect market integrity and ensure full adherence to UAE and international regulatory standards.

Do you accept buyers outside the UAE?

Yes. We serve private and institutional buyers across eligible global jurisdictions, subject to local regulations and successful due diligence.

Where can my holdings be vaulted?

We arrange introductions to accredited independent vaults in Panama and other major international financial hubs based on your custody preferences.

How is client confidentiality protected?

All client records and transaction details are handled with strict discretion under robust data-protection protocols, disclosed only where required by law.

What sets FKPA Global apart?

Over 50 years of combined industry expertise, direct refinery and wholesale networks, rigorous compliance, and integrated logistics and custody coordination.

How long does onboarding take?

Timelines depend on entity complexity and how quickly supporting documents are submitted, though our compliance team prioritizes prompt review.

Which metals and formats do you supply?

We source investment-grade physical gold, silver, and platinum bars and coins from accredited international refiners and mints.

Why hold physical bullion?

Buyers allocate to tangible metals for portfolio diversification, long-term wealth preservation, and zero counterparty risk. Suitability depends on your individual objectives and risk profile.

Do you offer investment advice or product guidance?

We provide factual product specifications, pricing, and logistics options to support your decision-making, but we do not offer financial, tax, or legal advice. Please consult an independent advisor regarding portfolio suitability.
