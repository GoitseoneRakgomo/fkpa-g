About Us

FKPA Global FZCO is an international physical precious metals company serving qualifying clients across eligible jurisdictions. We specialise in physical gold and silver transactions and in helping clients navigate the practical question that often follows an international precious metals purchase: where should I store it? Rather than limiting clients to a single storage destination, we help identify appropriate independent storage providers based on the client's individual requirements and facilitate the relevant introduction. For clients seeking storage in Panama, this may include Fort Kobbe Management Corp. Where another jurisdiction is more appropriate, we can assist in identifying alternative options. Our approach is private, personal and international.

A Central Point of Contact

Owning physical precious metals internationally can involve more than simply making a purchase. Where will the assets be held? How will they reach the chosen facility? Which storage jurisdiction is appropriate? FKPA Global provides a central point of contact throughout that process, purchase globally, store appropriately, maintain physical ownership.

International Precious Metals Storage

There is no single storage solution appropriate for every international client, so FKPA Global doesn't take a one-size-fits-all approach , we work with each client to understand their practical requirements before facilitating introductions to suitable independent storage providers.

Storage in Panama

Clients seeking secure private precious metals storage in Panama may be introduced to Fort Kobbe Management Corp., a separate Panama-based storage provider offering dedicated precious metals vaulting. The client establishes their storage relationship directly with Fort Kobbe, subject to Fort Kobbe's own onboarding, agreements and terms.

International Storage Options

Panama will not be the preferred storage jurisdiction for every client. Where a client's circumstances or preferences require another location, FKPA Global can assist in identifying and introducing alternative storage providers in appropriate jurisdictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I own the precious metals I purchase?

Yes. Clients retain ownership of the physical precious metals they acquire through FKPA Global, whether they choose secure storage or delivery, subject to the agreed transaction terms.

How do I get started with FKPA Global?

Simply contact our team to discuss your requirements. Before any transaction can proceed, all clients must successfully complete our onboarding, KYC, AML, and sanctions screening process.

Why do I need to complete KYC and AML checks?

As part of our strict compliance standards, all clients must complete Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification before we can conduct any business.

Does FKPA Global work with international clients?

Yes. We work with qualified private and institutional clients in various jurisdictions, subject to applicable regulations and successful compliance checks.

Where can my precious metals be stored?

We coordinate secure storage through approved vaulting facilities in selected jurisdictions, offering both domestic and international storage solutions.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We treat all client information with the highest level of confidentiality while adhering to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

What makes FKPA Global different?

With over 50 years of combined market experience, international sourcing capabilities, strict compliance standards, and secure logistics and storage solutions, FKPA Global provides a professional end-to-end precious metals service.

How long does the onboarding process take?

The timeframe varies depending on the completion of compliance checks and the documentation provided. Our team works to complete the process as efficiently as possible.

What types of precious metals are available?

We facilitate access to a range of physical precious metals, including gold, silver, platinum, and other approved precious metal products sourced through established international supply networks.

Why do clients acquire physical precious metals?

Clients acquire physical precious metals for a variety of reasons, including portfolio diversification, long-term wealth preservation, and ownership of tangible assets. The suitability of any purchase depends on each client's individual objectives, financial circumstances, and risk tolerance. FKPA Global provides information about its products and services but does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Can FKPA Global help me choose the right metals?

Yes. Our team works closely with clients to understand their objectives and assists with product selection, storage options, and transaction planning. We provide general product and service information only and do not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice or recommend any particular purchase. Clients should consider obtaining independent professional advice before making any purchasing decision.
